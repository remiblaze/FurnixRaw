# Furnix Raw — one-knob compressor

![Furnix Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/FurnixRaw/main/furnixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob. Turn it up and your track gets tighter, punchier, and louder.**

Furnix Raw is the free, one-knob version of **Furnix** (the full compressor). A single **SQUEEZE** macro drives the compressor internally and keeps the level matched for you — no threshold, ratio, or makeup to dial in.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/FurnixRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`FurnixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **SQUEEZE** knob is the whole plugin. Turn it up and it compresses harder while adding a touch of transient punch and analog heat — and because auto-gain is always on, you hear the groove get tighter and glued, never a jump in volume. Works on drums, bass, vocals, and the full mix.

That single knob is the only control (and the only parameter your DAW can automate). Everything else — threshold, ratio, attack, release, punch, and heat — is set for you behind the scenes.
