# Furnix Raw: one-knob compressor

![Furnix Raw free one-knob compressor UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/FurnixRaw/main/furnixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob. Turn it up and your track gets tighter, punchier, and louder.**

Furnix Raw is the free, one-knob version of **Furnix** (the full compressor). A single **SQUEEZE** macro drives the compressor internally and keeps the level matched for you, no threshold, ratio, or makeup to dial in.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/FurnixRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`FurnixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Furnix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`FurnixRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Furnix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **SQUEEZE** knob is the whole plugin. Turn it up and it compresses harder while adding a touch of transient punch and analog heat, and because auto-gain is always on, you hear the groove get tighter and glued, never a jump in volume. Works on drums, bass, vocals, and the full mix.

That single knob is the only control (and the only parameter your DAW can automate). Everything else, threshold, ratio, attack, release, punch, and heat, is set for you behind the scenes.
